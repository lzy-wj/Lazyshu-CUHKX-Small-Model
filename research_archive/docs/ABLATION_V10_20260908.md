# V10 Single-Variable Ablation Report

## Outcome

- Frozen production baseline remains V9: Kaggle public `0.82089`, fold-0 raw `71.0123%`, fold-0 decoded `76.8405%` (`501/652`).
- Three isolated fold-0 ablations were trained in parallel on GPUs 2, 3 and 7 with BF16 and SwanLab tracking.
- None passed the preregistered advancement gate, so fold 1, combination experiments and Kaggle submissions were intentionally not run.
- This round changed no final package or scored prediction.

## Frozen Protocol

- Validation users: fold 0 `[3, 8, 18, 23]`; fold 1 was hidden from model selection.
- Every candidate retained the seed, split, initialization policy, optimizer, schedule, TTA, V9 ensemble weights and repeat decoder.
- Advance only when standalone gains at least `0.5` percentage points without decoded regression, or decoded gains at least two samples without raw regression.
- The canonical scorer was `scripts/evaluate_single_ablation.py`; all candidate indices and labels matched the 652-row fold-0 reference.

## Findings

### Asynchronous Augmentation

- Independently shifting skeleton and IMU tokens by up to two positions is the only tested change that improved standalone accuracy (`+0.6135` pp).
- Its fixed-ensemble raw score and decoded score regressed, showing that the gain came with less complementary errors rather than a stronger final system.
- A four-position shift was too destructive. Any revisit should use lower-probability `±1/±2` shifts or train the final blend explicitly, not combine this checkpoint under frozen weights.

### Relative-Time Attention

- The fusion replacement added only 325 parameters while allowing directed modality-pair offsets and unequal token rates.
- Learned normalized offsets stayed near zero (`mean |offset| = 0.00124`, `max |offset| = 0.00535`), providing no evidence of useful cross-modal lag in the current temporally normalized cache.
- The unchanged decoder but lower raw scores reject this operator under the fixed training budget.

### Dynamic Person Crop

- Person detection covered `92.9999%` of sampled temporal positions; smoothing reduced center jitter by `33.7%` and retained `99.8535%` of selected boxes inside the crop.
- Despite technically stable tracking, visual initialization fell from `65.3374%` to `62.4233%`, and every downstream metric regressed.
- The likely failure mode is loss of stable body/scene context plus geometric motion introduced by resizing a changing box. The existing clip-level crop is the better frontend.

## Decision

- Keep `outputs/final_legal_multimodal_v9.zip` as the final verified package.
- Do not spend fold-1 compute on these candidates and do not combine failed single-variable changes.
- The next high-value experiment should be serial rather than another wide parallel sweep: preserve the fixed crop, keep the original fusion, and test a small-probability `±1/±2` synchronization regularizer with blend-aware checkpoint selection. It is the only direction in this round with a positive standalone signal.
