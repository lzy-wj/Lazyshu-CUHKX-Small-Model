# Results

The Fold 0 calibration sweep selected a head blend weight of 0.2. With that
fixed weight, V123 reached 1093/1357 raw and 1176/1357 pair-aware validation
accuracy. V114 reaches 1091/1357 raw and 1180/1357 pair-aware accuracy under
the same protocol. Target-weighted pair accuracy fell from 89.8048% to
89.2484%, so the extra raw classifications conflict with sequence structure and
the branch is rejected.
