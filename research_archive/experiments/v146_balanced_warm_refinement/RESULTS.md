# Results

Uniform class resampling reaches 477/652 and 549/705 raw, with 527/652 and
595/705 after the paired decoder. Both folds remain below their V23
initializations, and every nonzero fusion reduces Fold-0 V127 accuracy.

The official paper's random-split resampling gain does not transfer to this
warm-started cross-subject protocol. Formal selection remains V127 at
1,205/1,357.
