# Results

The fixed category evidence product reaches 566/652 on Fold 0, three rows below
V127. Target-weighted accuracy also falls from 89.30% to 88.54%.

The regression is concentrated in paired domains: unmatched-domain predictions
are unchanged, while stronger category confidence blocks useful source-script
corrections. V153 is rejected before Fold 1. This does not invalidate V151,
which applies the ontology only as training regularization rather than a hard
inference preference.
