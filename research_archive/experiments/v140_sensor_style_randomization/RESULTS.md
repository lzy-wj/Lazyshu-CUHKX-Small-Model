Gamma randomization preserves the Fold-0 raw accuracy of its V23
initialization but loses on Fold 1. Adding intensity jitter improves one
Fold-0 sequence result while remaining well below V127. Every tested route and
nonzero fusion weight reduces the Fold-0 final system, so formal selection
returns V127 unchanged at 1,205/1,357.

Sensor photometric style is not the dominant cross-user bottleneck. V141 moves
to labeled paired-person evidence in the frozen multimodal embedding space.
