# Results

Across ten deterministic partial-script views, V154 has deltas of
`[0,0,0,+1,+1]` on Fold 0 and `[-1,0,0,+1,0]` on Fold 1 relative to a freshly
decoded V127 baseline. Mean delta is +0.2 rows; nine views are nonnegative, but
only three improve and one regresses.

Triplicate-capacity matching is therefore robust to the local completeness
mismatch, but it does not provide enough consistent evidence to replace V127.
Its two-row test disagreement remains an audit candidate, not a submission.
