# V39 Diverse Compound

V39 combines four Fold-0-selected components that preserve their gain on the
blind Fold 1:

- V32 domain residual gate;
- V38 10% X3D-L correction below confidence 0.50;
- V36 5% skeleton auxiliary correction for visual-missing rows;
- V31 calibrated pair-aware decoder.

The R(2+1)D backbone, its small pseudo adapter, compact X3D-L, YOLO, and the
X3D-L person detector total about 88 MB.
